Everything you need to get the most out of iCountdownClock

📅Adding Events

  1. Open iCountdownClock on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the + button to add a new event.
  3. Enter a name and choose a date and time.
  4. Tap Save. Your event appears in the list immediately.

To edit or delete an event, swipe left on it in the list, or tap it to open the editor.

⌚Syncing to Your Apple Watch

Events sync to your Apple Watch automatically whenever you add or change them. Make sure your iPhone and Apple Watch are paired and within Bluetooth range. Open the watch app to see your events as scrollable tiles.

🕐Setting the Watch Face Complication

  1. In the iPhone app, tap the star (★) next to the event you want to feature on your watch face. This nominates it as your watch face event.
  2. On your Apple Watch, press and hold the watch face to enter edit mode.
  3. Tap Edit, then select a complication slot.
  4. Scroll to find iCountdownClock and select it.
  5. Your nominated event's countdown will now appear directly on the watch face.

💙HRV Stress Encouragement

iCountdownClock monitors your Heart Rate Variability (HRV) in the background using your Apple Watch. When it detects a dip in HRV — a sign of elevated stress — it sends you a gentle notification reminding you of something you're looking forward to, rather than telling you that you're stressed.

To enable this feature, allow iCountdownClock access to Heart Rate Variability data when prompted. You can review this permission at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices on your iPhone.

💲Unlocking Multiple Events

iCountdownClock is free for one event. To track unlimited events, purchase the Unlimited Events upgrade for a one-time payment of $2.99 — no subscription, ever.

To restore a previous purchase on a new device, tap Restore Purchase in the app settings.
